A pawn is passed when no opposing pawn is ahead of it on its file or either adjacent file. Forward is toward rank 8 for White and rank 1 for Black; let d = +1 for White and −1 for Black.

Validation & Playing Strength

The earlier Elo numbers were an unvalidated conversion of centipawn loss, not ratings measured through games. They have been withdrawn. Historical runs also used different Python and browser search rules, and the Python model labelled 3.5 selected the default checkpoint.

The current engine evaluates every legal candidate and immediate reply. Tactical assistance resolves captures and recaptures, then uses TabPFN for a bounded positional preference. Checkmates and draws receive exact scores. Disabling tactical assistance provides a model-only comparison.

The context scaling study uses nested samples of 100, 1,000 and 10,000 positions with identical model and search settings. Each context plays 400 games: 80 each against Stockfish 19 at UCI_Elo 1320, 1400, 1500, 1600 and 1800, using its documented 120 s + 1 s time control. The same 200 opening positions are played from both colors in every context. TabPFN uses fixed two-ply assisted search without a clock limit; its eight estimator KV caches are reused across all games and saved for restarts.

These are performance estimates relative to the configured opponents, not FIDE or online ratings. The combined estimate accounts for each opponent's Elo and uses completed opening pairs. Nominal 95% bounds resample whole White/Black pairs within each opponent setting; degenerate outcomes use conservative bounds instead. Early estimates remain uncertain. All wins or all losses have no finite point estimate. Games reaching the 600-ply cap are unfinished, withhold the paired point estimate and widen its bounds. Latency figures are earlier single-position pilots on a shared H100, excluding fit/cache preparation; they are not speed guarantees. The earlier eight-game pilot is excluded from this study. The tested context prefixes have no overlap with the opening roots or their immediate children. Deeper positions and original source games have not been checked for overlap. The 100,000-context run has been dropped.
